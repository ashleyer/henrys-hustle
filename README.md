# 🤸 Henry’s Hub Hustle

An action-packed 3D Subway Surfers style runner game centered in **Boston on the MBTA "T" subway system**, packed with Olympic gymnastics flips, vaults, custom character creator, and educational Hub history!

## 🌟 Highlights

- **Boston MBTA "The T" System**: Dodge parked and moving trains on the **Green Line**, **Red Line**, and **Orange Line**, sprint through historic Tremont Street tunnels, and leap across Boston rooftop tracks.
- **Historic Boston Stations & Milestones**:
  - **Park Street Station** (America’s very first subway station, opened September 1, 1897!)
  - **Boylston Station** (Historic downtown curves)
  - **Copley Square Station (02116)** (Boston Public Library & Marathon Finish Line)
  - **Kenmore & Fenway Park** (Home of the Green Monster & Citgo Sign)
  - **Charles / MGH** (Crossing the Longfellow Bridge over the Charles River)
  - **Harvard Square & Boston Harbor** (Tea Party history)
- **Educational Screen Time (The Hub & Gymnastics Academy)**:
  - **Boston History Trivia**: Discover why Boston is called "The Hub of the Universe", the 1773 Boston Tea Party, the Freedom Trail, and how Back Bay was reclaimed from tidal marshland.
  - **Gymnastics Academy**: Kid-friendly science explaining angular momentum in backflips, why gymnasts use magnesium carbonate chalk, and how to stick zero-hop landings.
- **Gymnastics Moves**:
  - **Olympic Vault**: Leap and vault cleanly over low hurdles and pommel blocks with gymnastic handsprings.
  - **Acrobatic Backflip**: 360° mid-air tuck flips and aerial cartwheels with judge multipliers.
  - **Forward Roll / Kip Slide**: Duck low and roll smoothly under high barriers, or dive down from mid-air.
  - **Olympic Balance Beam**: Balance-run across elevated suede beams between tracks with arms outstretched.
  - **Competition Springboards**: Launch sky-high off red & yellow gym springboards for big air trick combos!
- **Personalized Character Studio**:
  - Customize hair style (Spiky, Curly, Sweep, Ponytail, Buzz), hair color, skin tone, eye color, and headband.
  - Distinctive Boston & Gymnastics Outfits: *Boston T Conductor*, *Team Henry #7*, *Fenway Green Monster*, *Olympic Gold Champion*, *Freedom Trail Pioneer*, *Back Bay Sprinter 02116*, and *Neon Acrobat*.
- **Secure Google OAuth Login & Global Leaderboard**:
  - Sign in with Google to save your custom gymnast, unlocked leotards, and compete on the live Boston Leaderboard.
- **Interactive Tutorial**:
  - 5-step interactive gymnastics training gym for new players.
- **"H-E-N-R-Y" Word Hunt**:
  - Collect the 5 floating letters along the track to activate **Super Henry Hub Rush** (invincible speed, star magnet, and rainbow chalk trails!).
- **Ashley's Footer**:
  - Green Courier font with clickable contact modal to Email and GitHub repository.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch / Mobile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Change Lanes** | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` | Swipe Left / Right or On-Screen Arrows |
| **Jump / Vault** | `W` / `↑` / `Space` | Swipe Up or Green **VAULT** Button |
| **Forward Roll / Slide** | `S` / `↓` | Swipe Down or Orange **ROLL** Button |
| **Mid-Air Acrobatic Trick** | `E` / `F` or Jump in air | Center **GYMNASTICS TRICK** Button |
| **Chalk-Board Hoverboard** | `Shift` or Double-Tap | Double-Tap anywhere or **BOARD** Button |
| **Pause / Resume** | `P` / `Escape` | Pause Button in HUD |
