# 🤸 Henry’s Hustle

An action-packed 3D Subway Surfers style runner game created especially for **Henry (age 7)**, packed with city obstacles, subway trains, and **gymnastics moves**!

![Henry's Hustle](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ashleyer/henrys-hustle/main/preview.png)

## 🌟 Highlights

- **3D City Subway Environment**: Dodge parked and rolling metro trains, sprint across rooftops, slide under construction barriers, and leap along high tracks.
- **Gymnastics Moves**:
  - **Olympic Vault**: Leap and vault cleanly over low hurdles and pommel blocks with gymnastic hand-springs.
  - **Acrobatic Backflip**: 360° mid-air backflips and aerial cartwheels with score multipliers.
  - **Forward Roll / Kip Slide**: Duck low and roll smoothly under high barriers and signs.
  - **Balance Beam Sprint**: Balance-run across elevated suede beams between tracks with arms outstretched.
  - **Competition Springboards**: Launch sky-high off red & yellow gym springboards for big air trick combos!
- **"H-E-N-R-Y" Word Hunt**: Collect the 5 floating letters along the track to activate **Super Henry Fever Mode** (super speed, star magnetic pull, and invincibility).
- **Henry's Gym Wardrobe**: Choose from distinct gymnastics leotards & uniforms:
  - **Team Henry #7** (Classic Royal Blue & Gold)
  - **Olympic Gold** (Champion Gold & White)
  - **Neon Acrobat** (Cyber Teal & Purple)
  - **Super Gymnast** (Heroic Crimson & Gold)
- **Trophy Room & Olympic Judge Scores**: Difficulty and Execution scoring after every run with festive confetti.
- **Kid-Friendly Touch & Keyboard Controls**: Big responsive buttons and swipe detection on mobile/tablets, plus full keyboard support.
- **Procedural Audio**: Authentic Web Audio API synthesizer for lively background music and arcade sound effects with zero external audio file dependencies.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch / Mobile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Change Lanes** | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` | Swipe Left / Right or On-Screen Arrows |
| **Jump / Vault** | `W` / `↑` / `Space` | Swipe Up or Green **VAULT** Button |
| **Forward Roll / Slide** | `S` / `↓` | Swipe Down or Orange **ROLL** Button |
| **Gymnastics Trick** | `E` / `F` | Center **GYMNASTICS TRICK** Button |
| **Pause / Resume** | `P` / `Escape` | Pause Button in HUD |
